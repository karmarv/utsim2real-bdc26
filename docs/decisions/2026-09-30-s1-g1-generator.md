# 2026-09-30 G1 generator, first version: ray-cast walkers, Van mapping, calibrated background density

**Context.** Measured on the downloaded data:
- Neither UT dataset contains a walking pedestrian. UT-LUMPI has no Person class (Person is 1/7 of the LUMPI score); UT-V2X has riders only (pedestrian is 62% of the V2X test objects).
- LUMPI id 6 (the challenge "Van", see `data-lumpi-track/README.md` Section 5) has van dimensions on legal Measurement4 frames: median 4.84 x 1.99 x 1.95 m (Car: 4.48 x 1.99 x 1.70 m).
- Uniformly resampled UT-LUMPI frames score R = 0.596 on our legal proxy (the ECCV winner reported about 0.59 for random raw UT-LUMPI frames, so the proxy is calibrated). The UT twin lacks static structure the real sensors see (parked-car rows, vegetation) and over-represents near-sensor ground rings: FPD 61 (term pinned at 1), EMD 2.8 m.
- UT-LUMPI frames were simulated with the real Measurement4 rig in `meta.json`: assigning each UT point to the sensor whose beam table explains it gives a median elevation residual of 0.03 deg, and per-sensor point shares match real frames (61/23/7/4/5% vs 63/20/2/7/9%).

**Decision.**
1. **Walkers** (`common/bdc26/render/pedsim.py`, `g1.py`): articulated ellipsoid bodies, ray-cast analytically with each sensor's real beam table and azimuth step (LUMPI: the five M4 lidars from `meta.json`; V2X: OS1-64 specification at the frame origin, 1.5x columns to match the organizers' resampling density). Insertion is ray-consistent: frame points behind a walker are removed by an exact ray test, and walker returns hidden behind frame geometry are dropped with a per-sensor range image. LUMPI walkers copy the position, heading and box size of a random legal M4 Person label row (jittered), and the body is fitted inside that box. Check on legal data: inserted Persons carry a median of 47 points at 50k (real M4 Persons: 42); by range 49/61/24 vs 61/63/28 points at 10-20/20-30/30-45 m. V2X walkers use free observed ground and a KITTI-like box convention until legal V2X labels are unlocked.
2. **UT Van -> Van** for LUMPI (g1 datasets), instead of folding vans into Car.
3. **Calibrated background density** (`common/bdc26/density.py`): a coarse 3D ratio (real / synthetic point density, 8 x 8 x 2.5 m cells) fitted on 400 legal unlabeled realval-A frames that are disjoint from all realism proxy sets, and 400 frames of the generator's own uniform output. At resampling, object points keep the uniform rate and background points are kept with probability proportional to the ratio. Cell size was chosen on proxies 1-3 (R 0.596 -> 0.884/0.880/0.874); proxies 4-5 were held out for the final estimate.
4. The setting is part of the generator: the training corpus (`pcdet/g1-v2`) and the submitted frames (its val split) come from the same configuration. No real point is copied; the test frames are never involved; no affine or quantile warping of coordinates.

**Classification.** Items 1 and 2 use sensor metadata and label statistics (allowed, `01` Section 3.1). Item 3 uses unlabeled real frames of the train split to adapt the generator's sampling, which we read as "domain adaptation" under the Rules tab. It is close to the gray row "generator fitted to real scans", so it is **disclosed in the report and should be in the organizer email** (`01` Section 10). If the organizers object, set `--density-prior` off (`pcdet/g1-v1` is the uncalibrated corpus) and resubmit.

**Consequences.** Models `model-lumpi-track/archive/pointpillars-g1` (data `g1-v2`) and `model-v2x-track/archive/pointpillars-g1` (data `g1-v1`; V2X calibration waits for legal real V2X frames). The LUMPI baseline `pointpillars-ut` run was stopped at epoch 4 to free GPUs for G1; rerun it later as the ablation.

**Update (same day): walker v2.** The first G1 checkpoint (epoch 11, untuned) found 97% of synthetic Persons but only 28% of real ones on realval-A (score >= 0.1, BEV IoU 0.25), even for well-observed real Persons (30+ points: 25%). Real Person clusters are round blobs; the v1 walkers were flat. Measured on legal realval-A Persons with 20-200 points, the horizontal std of the torso band (0.3-1.6 m) along / across the box heading is 0.125 / 0.123 m for real, 0.068 / 0.132 m for v1. Walker v2 (`data-lumpi-track/prepare_g1.py` preset `v2`: deeper torsos, stronger arm swing, bags, body yaw scatter 0.8 rad, body top 2-14 cm below the box top, 4 cm per-sensor misregistration offsets, 3 cm range noise) gives 0.121 / 0.122 m. New corpus `pcdet/g1-v3`, model `model-lumpi-track/archive/pointpillars-g1v3`; it is compared with `pointpillars-g1` on realval-A/B and the better one is submitted. The LUMPI baseline ablation was deferred again to give `g1v3` the GPUs.

**Update (2026-10-02): V2X calibration on legal real data.** After the starting kit resolved the V2X guard: (a) legal walker label rows (70,818 Pedestrian/Child/RoadWorker boxes on guard-allowed frames) give walker placement, box sizes and ground heights (`gen/walker_prior_v1.npz`); (b) 23 legal unlabeled frames give the OS1-64 beam table (`gen/os1_64_calib_v1.npz`: rows +16.13 to -16.77 deg; the bins are in the sensor's own frame); (c) UT points outside that field of view are masked (UT-V2X emulates a sensor reaching -56 deg); (d) the azimuth density gain (2.5) was chosen so walker point counts match real walkers at every range (52/40/18/7 vs 46/36/19/7). Classification: sensor measurement and label statistics, both in the allowed rows of `01` Section 3.1; no real point is copied. Result: realval-A pedestrian AP 0.173 -> 0.266; realism 0.901 -> 0.919. Corpus `pcdet/g1-v3`, model `model-v2x-track/archive/pointpillars-g1v3`.

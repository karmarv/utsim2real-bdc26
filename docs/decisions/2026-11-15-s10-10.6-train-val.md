## 3. LUMPI: L16 (running)

- **Corpus** `pcdet/g1-v6tv`: points and labels are symlinks to `g1-v6`; `ImageSets/train.txt` = the 9,000 train ids +
  the 1,000 val ids; infos and the GT database rebuilt (10,000 frames; about 10% more objects per class: Person 159,460,
  Car 240,352, Truck 53,918, Van 17,611). `g1-v6` itself is unchanged.
- **Members retrained** (parent config and seed 666, 4 GPUs each, started 20:00 UTC): `voxelrcnn-rosf-g1v6tv` (Car's
  direct member and draw 1 of Person's bag in L12e) and `voxelrcnn-rosn-g1v6tv` (Person's direct member and draw 1 of
  Car's bag). New bags with the retrained draw 1: `bag-voxelrcnn-rosf-g1v6tvx`, `bag-voxelrcnn-rosn-g1v6tvx`.
- **Tuning:** the standard member post-processing (plain, 4-view) and 12-view tuning jobs (L12e's Person and Car fusions
  use 12-view members), queued on GPUs 3 and 7 after each training.
- **L16** = L12e with the four models replaced (`s10_5_build.sh replace`): same keys, views, route, fusion sets, IoUs,
  weights and stages; validate; the per-class final check against L12e (`s7_check`; failing classes frozen at L12e);
  build with L12e's frames (kit predictions of the new members alone on an idle GPU; kit check; audit; re-merge; box
  budget); upload rule against L8.

## 4. V2X: evaluated, not run

- The analogous change would retrain V1's / V4's Voxel R-CNN members on V2X `g1-v6` train + val. One V2X Voxel R-CNN
  retraining takes about 3 h on 4 GPUs; all 8 GPUs are on the LUMPI retraining until about 00:10 UTC; with tuning (about
  1 h) and the build (about 1 h) a V2X system would land about 05:10 UTC, **after the CodaBench close (04:59 UTC)**.
  Running it alongside LUMPI would slow both past the close.
- V2X is already rank 1 (0.894399, +0.0069 over the next entry). **V4 stays the V2X entry; no V2X upload in 10.6.**

## 5. Rules (fixed now, before any 10.6 result)

1. Every rule of the 10.5 record Sections 4 and 12: synthetic-only training (`clean_check.py` rule 1), frames from the
   detector's training data (rule 1b), kit check, audit 6/6, re-merge identical, LUMPI box budget 38,466.
2. A class changes only through the per-class final check against L12e (pooled P(gain) >= 0.9, neither split down more
   than 0.005, Person late-A not down).
3. **L16 is the upload only if** it passes rule 14 against L8 **and** rule 14 against its parent L12e says GO, or
   JUDGEMENT with a pooled delta above 0 (the 10.5 final selection rule). Otherwise L12e stays the entry and the last
   LUMPI upload is not used.
4. Nothing is chosen from a server number; nothing is uploaded or pushed by us.

**Expectation:** small. 1,000 more frames of the same generator draw is an 11% increase in data; the 10.2 bag experiments
saturated with whole new draws. The most likely outcome is "no L16" (every class within seed noise, frozen at L12e); if a
class passes, a gain of about +0.0002 to +0.0005 combined. Reaching 0.5440 needs +0.0010: unlikely.

# 2026-10-14 rules: compliance review of every method and real-data use against the Rules, Terms and declaration

**Version.** All strategies S1-R3 as submitted, and the planned R5 steps (`02-STRATEGY.md` Section 8.9).

**Context.** Before R5 and the final phase, every method was checked against the binding texts as published on 2026-10-14 (CodaBench 16684/16685 Rules and Terms through the public API, the challenge site, the kit's `declaration_template.txt`). New since the Oct 2 check: Letters of Intent are accepted until Oct 30 (late entrants are possible), and Terms item 6: top-ranked Final-phase teams "agree to be contacted to verify their submission's integrity".

**The binding texts.** (1) Declaration in every zip (falsifying it: disqualification and removal from the proceedings): "I did not train on any frame listed in forbidden_frames.txt, and ... all submitted detections were produced by a model trained only on synthetic data I generated." (2) Rules, Allowed: "Using the train split of the public real dataset for hyperparameter selection or domain adaptation, provided no forbidden frame is touched." (3) Rules, Forbidden: "Training, validating, hyperparameter-tuning, or otherwise observing ground truth from any of the 100 frames"; "predictions produced by a model that has been trained on real labels for any roadside-LiDAR dataset. The detector must be sim-only"; "inferring the held-out realism reference frames via Codabench's leaderboard signal (e.g., probing submissions ...)". (4) Site: forbidden "Using real data for training, including fine-tuning"; development phase "Use this to iterate"; final phase "resubmit your best system".

**Reading.** The texts agree if "domain adaptation" covers everything except the detector's trained parameters: the generator, hyperparameters and post-processing may adapt to the real train split, while the detector's weights come from synthetic data only (declaration, site, "sim-only"). This reading is not stricter than any text requires, and it is the one we apply.

| Background occupancy map from real frames at inference | **not done** (gray) | the submitted detections would no longer come from the sim-trained model alone; only with a written ruling |

**Decision.** Continue under this reading. R5 adds no new kind of real-data use: EMD-targeted frame selection uses legal proxies only; `g1-v5` takes geometry from public maps; the Person selection rule uses legal label provenance; seeds and walker copies use real data only for validation. `01-CHALLENGE-FACTS.md` Section 3.1 is updated to match this table.

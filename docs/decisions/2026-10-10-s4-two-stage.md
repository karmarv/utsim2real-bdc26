**Context.** The S3 review showed that V2X pedestrians lose 0.27-0.33 AP and LUMPI Persons 0.18-0.29 AP to localization (found at 3D IoU 0.25, not at 0.5), as noise rather than bias. Over the five uploads with a local change (S1+C1, S3 and S4), the leaderboard gain was 0.25-1.74 x the A gain and 0.48-1.90 x the B gain, but its sign was right every time. Only the gated classes changed on the leaderboard (LUMPI: Bicycle alone).

**Consequences.** Leaderboard projection revised to previous leaderboard mAP + 0.6 x the mean of the A and B gains (median of the five uploads; spread 0.4-1.5 x), replacing the S3 rule fitted on two uploads; the gates stay on B, which also judged the LUMPI S4 gain right (estimate +0.003, leaderboard +0.0025). Walker localization is not a detector problem on synthetic data: the next walker levers are generator content (static hard negatives, R3) and label-convention statistics of real walkers; S5 (geometry-invariant inputs) should be judged on its walker localization loss. Two-stage members stay in the portfolio for vehicles, trucks and bicycles; every new member enters as plain and TTA variants with a per-class score blend, and `fuse --max-members 3` with incumbent-aware selection is the default from S4 on.

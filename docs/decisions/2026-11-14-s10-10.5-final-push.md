## 4. Rules fixed now (before any 10.5 result)

1. **Every system:** rules 1 and 1b; kit check (valid, under 1200 s); upload audit 6 of 6 (frames byte-identical to
   the parent's zip for L9 and V5); re-merge identical; LUMPI at most 38,466 boxes.
2. **View-set swap, per class** (L9, V5): a class takes the 12-view siblings of all its 4-view members only if
   (a) its pooled realval A+B AP gains with paired-bootstrap P(gain) >= 0.9, (b) neither A nor B drops by more than
   0.005, (c) on LUMPI its late-A AP does not drop. No class passes: no system.
3. **L9 and V5 upload rule:** rule 14 (`s10_grouped_gate.py`) against the parent (L8, V4) says GO, or JUDGEMENT with a
   pooled realval mAP delta above 0.
4. **V3xp upload rule** (fixed in the 10.4 record before any 10.4 result, unchanged): the pooled G-D mAP delta of V3x
   against V1 is above -0.0027.
5. **V3p** goes to the entrant only if V3xp is not packaged by 22:30 UTC and V3p passes rule 1.
6. If a phase-B 12-view tuning job of L9 is not done by 14:30 UTC, L9's class rule runs on the classes that have
   12-view outputs at that time.

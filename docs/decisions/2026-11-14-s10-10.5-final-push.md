### 10.2 The arm: TransFusion-L as a new member family

- **Why this head.** TransFusion-L (Bai et al., CVPR 2022) replaces the per-class-group CenterHead with one set of
  object queries initialised from a class-aware heat map and matched jointly to all classes (Hungarian assignment,
  transformer decoder); on nuScenes it is +5 mAP over CenterPoint with the same backbone. It is the one published
  joint-class head installed in our OpenPCDet (0.6.0, 233f849) that no LUMPI member uses. The stronger published
  backbones (HEDNet, SAFDNet, LION) are not installed and need downloads; DSVT (installed, 6 members) takes about 7 h.
- **Member** `transfusion-rosf-g1v6`: `voxelrcnn-rosf-g1v6`'s backbone, data config and corpus (`pcdet/g1-v6`, the
  submission frames' corpus), 40 epochs, seed 666, 8 GPUs (batch 32, LR 0.001: the nuScenes recipe's batch and LR),

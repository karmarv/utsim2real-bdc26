## 5. Gates (fixed now, before any result)

1. **G-D (detection), per system.** The retrained system against its parent on realval A and B: rule 14's grouped bootstrap (`s10_grouped_gate.py`) and, on LUMPI, late-A (`s10_3_compare.py`).
   - The 90% CI enters the projection.
   - A system goes to the entrant if its projection is above the best qualifying entry: LUMPI 0.5408, V2X 0.8913.
2. **Rules 1 and 1b** (`clean_check.py`): no real label row; the frames come from a training corpus of the shipped checkpoints, and each calibration on it is in every checkpoint's training data.
3. **Box budget (LUMPI):** at most 38,466 boxes.
   - The Person copy threshold is re-derived for the route, as in `C0`'s recipe (S9 rule 1(b)).
   - L6's P copies are counted inside the budget.
4. **Kit check** (official scorer, legal proxy): valid, under 1200 s.
5. **Upload audit** (`s10_2_audit_upload.py`), 6 of 6: md5; zip contents and declaration; rules 1 and 1b; frames byte-identical to the corpus; ids in the corpus splits; L6's frames byte-identical to L3's.
6. **Reproduction:** re-merge identical from the saved member predictions.
7. **No server number** chooses a frame set, a knob, a route or a member.

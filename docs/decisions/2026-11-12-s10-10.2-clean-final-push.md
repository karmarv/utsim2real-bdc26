## 3. What the research says, and what follows from it

**The ECCV edition of this challenge** (same scorer, same data; docs/07 Section 2):
- **amanupg** (1st on both tracks): post-processing levers (per-class NMS, re-centring, size calibration, density re-ranking, duplicate emission). Its realism came from frame selection, density resampling, (0,0,0) nulls, and affine moment alignment tuned with server probes. Server probes are not open to us. Their report: "EMD holds 79-86% of the remaining realism headroom", and 35% of the LUMPI reference mass lies where the twin has no geometry.
- **luopuu** (2nd on V2X): 50k resampling for honest validation; RangeLDM redraws as an extra training domain; DSVT experts with per-class fusion; synthetic pedestrians resized to real dimensions. Their warning: "fancy realism transforms fail on the hidden set".
- **A public LUMPI entry:** realism fitted on the forbidden test frames fell from 0.906 to 0.593 when refitted legally. A realism gain must be fitted on legal frames and judged on held-out ones.

**Literature** (docs/07 Section 3):
- Density-aware training data helps cross-domain detection: PDDA (ECCV 2024, physics-aware density resampling) and DTS (CVPR 2023, random beam re-sampling).
- So retraining on density-calibrated corpora **may raise detection as well as realism**. It is not measured for our data, and the detection gate decides.

## 5. The two calibrated generators, specified

**LUMPI (lever B), applied in this order to every frame of the training corpora.**
1. **The PandarQT near-field re-cast.** `common/tools/s10_beamfix.py`, exactly as for `g1-v6pq` (sensor 36, radius 7 m, beam table from the Measurement4 metadata). `g1-v6pq` already exists; `g1-v6d2` (and `d3`, `d4` for L4) get the same pass.
2. **N1, the 2 x 2 x 0.5 m density prior**, as researched (S10 record 15.16, research3 `safe_frames`):
   - the 12,601 per-voxel weights fitted on proxies 1-3 (Measurement4 frames 40-6390, unlabelled, legal) by nearest-support assignment;
   - no weight clip;
   - each frame re-drawn to 50,000 points from its own points by systematic sampling;
   - repeated points get N(0, 2 cm) jitter.
   - **Box guard:** points inside labelled boxes stay as generated, so labels and object points are unchanged. It is dropped only if it loses more than 0.0015 R in gate G-R (pre-registered in S10 15.17). **Result: dropped** (Section 11). The guarded re-draw lost 0.0138 R against the unguarded one: it keeps the synthetic objects' excess points (18% of a frame), which is the excess N1 corrects. The corpora are built unguarded. Labels are unchanged; object points are re-drawn like all others.
3. **New corpora:**
   - `g1-v6n1` (from `g1-v6pq`) and `g1-v6d2n1`;
   - `g1-v6jn1`, a union card of the two (no new points);
   - for L4, `g1-v6d3n1` and `g1-v6d4n1`.

   Each corpus gets its infos and GT database. Its card's `real_data_use` adds the two calibration items and their fit frames, so rule 1b can check them. About 8 GB per corpus.
4. **The 50 frames:** C0's 50 ids, taken from `g1-v6n1`. No new frame selection.

**V2X (lever V3), applied to every frame of the training corpora.**
1. **`aw_mid`.**
   - W: per (azimuth, elevation) bin of 10 x 2.125 deg, our ranges are quantile-mapped onto the real ones (21 quantiles, fitted on V2X proxies 1-3); points move only along their own rays.
   - A: the share of points per bin is rebalanced (gamma 0.5) with background points from other `g1-v6` frames outside the 50.
   - Points in boxes are untouched.
2. **The no-returns in the corpus.** Each frame is stored as the packaged frame would be (50,000 points, 31% at (0,0,0)). The loader strips them as before.
3. **New corpora:** `g1-v6aw` and `g1-v6d2aw` (the corpora `c1a`'s 4 checkpoints use); the 50 ids from `g1-v6aw`.

**Copy audit for both** (before any training). No output point lies within 1 mm of a real fit point beyond the base rate, and every output point is a point of its own synthetic frame (or another synthetic frame, for A), possibly moved along its ray or jittered. The report states the result.

## 7. Gates, fixed now before any result

1. **G-R, the realism gate for B (CPU, before any training).** The 50 ids from `g1-v6n1` against C0's frames. Judges: proxies 4, 5, late-3, b-m5 and b-m6, at scorer seeds 1234 and 99 (10 numbers).
   - **Pass:** mean dR at least +0.006 with 9 of 10 wins, CD below 2.0, MMD and FPD not worse.
   - **Fail:** B stops, and its GPU time goes to V3, then to more depth for L2.
   - For V3 the same gate applies on V2X proxies 4 and 5 at seeds 1234 and 2024 (4 numbers): at least +0.006, 4 of 4.
2. **G-D, the detection gate (realval A/B, after training).** The retrained system against the system it copies (L3 against `C0`, L4 against `c2r`, V3 against `c1a`), with rule 14's grouped bootstrap.
   - It decides what the record calls the detection change, and its 90% CI enters the projection. It cannot veto a system whose combined projection is above the best expected valid score.
   - Projection = rule 6 detection projection + 0.4 x 0.70 x the held-out realism gain (central). The low band (0.31x) is reported beside it.
3. **G-1, every package.** Rule 1 (no real label), rule 1b (frames from the training data), the R1 interlock, `declaration.txt`, the copy audit (calibrated frames).
4. **G-K.** The official kit check (format, 50 frames, run time under 1200 s).
5. **The upload rule.** A candidate that passes G-1 and G-K, and whose projection is at least the best valid entry's expected score at that time, is uploaded in the order it becomes ready. Only complete systems are compared, by combined score. **No server number chooses a frame set, a knob, a class route or a member.**
